FeedFinder Studio

A private tool that edits short countdown videos and posts them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Collects short clips only from sources whose licence allows reuse: Creative Commons (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0), the public domain, or a free library licence that allows edited reuse (Pexels, Pixabay). Every clip is downloaded through its source's own official download. No content is ever taken from YouTube.
  • Cuts one moment from each of three or four clips into a vertical countdown Short ("Top 3" or "Top 4"), and credits every clip the way its licence asks: title, author, link, licence, and that it was edited.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API, with a publish time, at most three a day. It does this only after the owner has given their go-ahead for that channel, and stops the moment it is withdrawn.
  • Reads the owner's own videos' statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) to learn which kinds of video people enjoy.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of the channels it posts to (@FeedFinder_official, and a test channel). It runs on the owner's own computer. It has no other users, no accounts, and no public interface.

FeedFinder Studio uses YouTube API Services.
