What this is

FeedFinder Studio is a private desktop tool used by its owner to edit short videos from openly licensed clips and post them to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube

FeedFinder Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Its use of data is described in the Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent to posting

FeedFinder Studio posts to a channel only after its owner has given an explicit go-ahead for that channel, which states what it allows: uploading up to three finished videos a day with publish times, one credit comment on each, and deleting one of them when vetoed. The owner can withdraw the go-ahead at any time, and posting stops from that moment.

Content and credit

Clips are used only under licences that allow it (Creative Commons BY, BY-SA, CC0, public domain, or the Pexels and Pixabay licences), and each is credited in the video's description as its licence requires. A creator who wants their clip removed can say so in a comment on the video, and the video is taken down.
